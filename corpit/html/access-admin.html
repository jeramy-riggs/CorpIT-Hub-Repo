Access Control

Roles decide what a group of people can reach. A role's default applies to every page that has no explicit setting, so it is the field that decides what a newly added page inherits. Per-person settings override the role in both directions — grant above it, or deny below it.

Per-person settings are the exception hatch. Deny holds someone below their role — the only way to take one page away from a single person without changing the whole role. Grant lifts them above it. Everything else stays on Default, which follows the role and keeps following it as roles change.
