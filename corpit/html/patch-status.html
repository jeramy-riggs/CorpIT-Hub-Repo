Waiting for the first read

Every number is a device's last report

Intune only knows what a device said at its last check-in. A device that has not checked in may already be fixed; open it and look at Last check-in before acting.

Needs attention lists what to work, most serious first. Each item opens the list of devices behind it. Click a device name for everything Intune holds on it, its Defender state and discovered apps.

Housekeeping finds records to clean up: devices that stopped checking in, duplicate records for one serial number, and Entra device records with no Intune record. Nothing on this page deletes or retires a device; each row opens the record in Intune or Entra, where the action is taken and audited.

Update rings and compliance policies are read from Intune on every refresh: which ring each Windows PC is in, what it last reported for that ring, and which policy and setting a non-compliant device fails. Apps lists the apps Intune deploys with failed installs; click one for the devices and the error code. Enrollment & Autopilot compares the Autopilot hardware list with Intune by serial number.

GPO migration: the shared register stores owners, stages, existing profile links, pilot groups and review evidence in CorpIT. It does not create policies or change assignments, group membership or GPOs.

In-page changes: super-admins can review and save update-ring edits in Intune, or review and run the pinned enrollment repair through ManageEngine on up to five eligible PCs. The hub audits both actions. Other administrative changes open the appropriate portal.

Ring target dates estimate timing from the baseline release date and ring settings. Actual enforcement depends on the device discovering the update and reaching a pending restart; those times are not read here.

Patch status is Windows only: Current is at or past the baseline update, Pending is behind it and checked in within 7 days, Not checking in is behind with no check-in for over 7 days. ManageEngine patching runs alongside Intune; this page cannot tell which tool installed an update.
