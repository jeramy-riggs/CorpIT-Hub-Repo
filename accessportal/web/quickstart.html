The six request forms
🌐
Out-of-Country Access
OCA
What it covers

Temporary authorization to access company systems, VPN, and SaaS applications while physically located outside the United States.

When to submit
  • International business trips
  • Extended remote work from abroad
  • Conference, client site, or project travel
  • Submit at least 5 business days before departure
What you'll need
  • Destination country and travel dates
  • Systems and applications you need access to
  • Business purpose / trip justification
  • Whether VPN and MFA are configured
Approval workflow
1🏢 Dept. Manager
2🔒 Security & Compliance
3👥 Human Resources
4⚖️ Legal
⏱ Typical: 3–5 business days 4 approvers
🤖
AI Use Case Intake
AIA
What it covers

Formal intake process for proposing any new AI tool, LLM, copilot, or automation agent for use at Auction.com — whether free, licensed, or custom-built.

When to submit
  • Before trialing any new AI tool with company data
  • Expanding an approved AI tool to new use cases
  • Building or deploying a custom AI/ML agent
  • Any AI integration touching ADC systems or clients
What you'll need
  • Tool name, vendor, and use case description
  • Audience (internal / customers / clients)
  • Data sensitivity level
  • Whether the tool integrates with ADC or client systems
  • Human review process for AI outputs
Approval workflow
1🏢 Dept. Manager
2🔒 Security & Compliance
3⚖️ Legal
⏱ Typical: 2–4 business days 3 approvers
🌍
Website / URL Access
WUA
What it covers

Request to unblock a specific website, domain, or URL category that is filtered by Zscaler on the company network.

When to submit
  • A business-critical site is blocked by the proxy
  • A vendor or client portal is inaccessible
  • A software download source is being filtered
  • A research or reference site needed for work
What you'll need
  • The exact URL or domain to unblock
  • URL category (if known)
  • How long you need access (temporary or permanent)
  • Clear business justification
Approval workflow
1🏢 Dept. Manager
2🔒 Security & Compliance
⏱ Typical: 1–2 business days 2 approvers · Fastest
🔐
Privileged / Admin Access
PAA
What it covers

Request for elevated or administrative access to systems, servers, cloud environments, or infrastructure beyond standard user permissions.

When to submit
  • Needing root/admin rights on a server or device
  • Cloud console access (AWS, Azure, GCP)
  • Database or production environment access
  • Network device or firewall administrative access
What you'll need
  • Access level required (read-only, admin, root)
  • Systems or environments involved
  • Duration (days)
  • Detailed business justification and scope
Approval workflow
1🏢 Dept. Manager
2🔒 Security & Compliance
3👥 Human Resources
4⚖️ Legal
⏱ Typical: 3–5 business days 4 approvers
🛡️
Security Exception Request
SER
What it covers

Formal request for a documented exception to an ADC information security policy or standard security control. Requires executive-level approval.

When to submit
  • A required security control blocks a business-critical process
  • A legacy system cannot meet current security standards
  • A time-limited exception is needed during a transition
  • A vendor or partner requires a deviation from policy
What you'll need
  • Specific policy or control being excepted
  • Scope — systems, users, and data affected
  • Business justification and risk acknowledgment
  • Exception expiry date and compensating controls
  • Frequency of use and sensitive data exposure level
Approval workflow
1🏢 Dept. Manager
2🔐 Information Security
3⚖️ Legal
4💻 CTO
5🏛️ CEO
⏱ Typical: 5–10 business days 5 approvers · Executive chain
🚨
IT Security Incident Form
SIR
What it covers

Formal documentation of a security incident — unauthorized access, data exposure, malware, phishing, or any event requiring investigation and official record.

When to submit
  • Suspected or confirmed breach of data or systems
  • Unauthorized account access or privilege escalation
  • Malware, ransomware, or phishing incident
  • Physical security event affecting IT assets
  • Submit immediately — do not delay reporting
What you'll need
  • Incident date, time, and criticality level
  • Executive summary of what occurred
  • Root cause (if known)
  • Systems and services impacted
  • Action steps already taken
  • Security implications and RCA findings

Request statuses explained
Pending — waiting for an approver to act
Approved — all approvers signed off
Rejected — denied at one or more steps
Returned — sent back for corrections or more info
Draft — saved but not yet submitted
Tips for faster approvals
Submit early

Multi-step approvals can take days. Submit OCA and PAA requests at least 5 business days before you need access.

Complete all fields

Incomplete or vague submissions get returned. A clear business justification is the single most common reason requests are held up.

Save your request ID

Every submission gets an ID (e.g. OCA-2026-00001). Use the "Track My Request" link on the portal home to check status anytime.

Manager approves first

Every form routes to your Dept. Manager first. A quick heads-up to your manager before submitting can cut days off the process.

Returned ≠ rejected

A "Returned" request means an approver needs more detail. Respond to the comments, update your submission, and resubmit.

Security incidents: don't wait

SIR forms must be submitted immediately upon discovering an incident. Delayed reporting escalates both risk and liability.
